he emerged from his game-day basement, eyes fraught with gloomy wonder of the final championship. outside the sun was blasting cancer beams and house music. this made bobby knight squint, frown with generation gap.

knight strolled down the cul-de-sac with his lab, buckeye, till, through his tracksuit, he felt the fangs of the sun.
