It isn't instantaneous what happens to a boy stuck in a field of light. The brightness saturating everything. Whiteness without end. If he's strong enough he'll become static. A frequency of light and darkness.

The light path cools and fades. A man seizes and screams on the cold metal floor. His mind hasn't caught up.
