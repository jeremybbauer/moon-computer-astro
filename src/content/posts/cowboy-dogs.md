I tried to fill your trailer with glass lilies but stopped, seeing the shark fetus jar. Something didn't feel right. So I took a drink of Yoohoo-infused gin and examined the fetus. It looked gummy. It looked like it wanted teeth pressed into it and also to complete its yawn.

I grabbed the green bowl and set it on the counter, waiting for the cowboy dogs to come back from wherever they'd gone. The trailer smelled like static and cut grass.
